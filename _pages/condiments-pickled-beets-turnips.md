## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/condiments/condiments-pickled-onion-02.jpg)

1. Beet (half)
1. Turnip (2)
1. Boiling Water (2 cups)
1. Vinegar (0.5 cup)
1. Kosher Salt (half tbsp)
1. Garlic (1 tsp)



## Instructions

1. Boil water, vinegar and salt 
1. Peel and cut beets and turnips
1. Add garlic to the bottom of the jar and layer beets and turnips
1. Cover with boiling mix and ensure everything is covered
1. After cooling on a countertop, regrigerate for 5 days

## Modifications

1. Other spices (garlic, peppers) for different pickling tastes
