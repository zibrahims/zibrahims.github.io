## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. 2 Turnips
1. 1 Beet
1. Hot Water (5 cups)
1. Vinega (1 1/4 cups: 25% of Hot Water)
1. Kosher Salt (3 tbsp: 3/5 tbsp of water)
1. Optional spices
   * Garlic
   * Chillis

## Instructions

1. Boil water and salt
1. Peel and cut turnips and beets into sticks
1. Place them evenly in jar
1. Add salted water + vinegar
1. Add optional spices
1. Wait 1 week

## Modifications

1. Add garlic and chillis for flavour
