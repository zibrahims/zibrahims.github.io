## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Spaghetti or Acorn Squash
1. Oil
1. Spices
   * Salt
   * Pepper

## Instructions

1. Cut squash in half, deseed and cover in oil, salt and pepper
1. Spaghetti squash (400F), Acorn squash (425F)
   1. Cook with flesh side down (skin side up) and oiled
1. Cook until golden brown (25 to 40 min)
1. For Acorn squash, cook at 450F for 10 min extra to make super soft

## Modifications

1. Sautee onions on the side and toss them into the boiling water
1. Add green chillis and parsley at the end
