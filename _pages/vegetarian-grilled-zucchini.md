## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Zucchini
1. Bread Crumbs
1. Shallots
1. Butter
1. Parsley

## Instructions

1. Grill sliced zucchini with salt and pepper for 5 min
1. In a separate pan, brown the bread crumbs. Add shallots to sautee once done.
1. Mix with zucchini
