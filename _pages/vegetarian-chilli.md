## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Black and pinto beans (each 1/2 cup dry)
1. 2 cups vegetable broth
1. Vegetables
   * Onions
   * 1 large can of diced tomatoes
   * Red Pepper
   * Carrots
   * Celery
1. Spices
   * salt
   * 1 bay leaf
   * 4 cloves garlic
   * 2 tbsp chilli
   * 2 tsp cumin
   * 1.5 tsp paprika
   * 1 tsp oregano
   * Cumin
   * Green Chillis (whole for kids, slitted for adults)
1. Optional
   * Browned ground beef or impossible

## Instructions

1. Sautee the chopped onions, pepper, carrot, celery with salt
1. Add all the spices + oil once the water is boiling
1. Add diced tomatoes, broth, bayleaf, overnight beans and "beef"
1. Remove the bay leaf, blend 1/4 of the chilli and drop back in
1. Add garnish
