## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Rice Flakes
1. Thai Basil
1. Garlic
1. Carrots
1. Thai Chillies
1. Sauce
   1. Oyster Sauce (2 tbsp)
   1. Fish Sauce (1/2 tbsp)
   1. Soy Sauce (1 tbsp)
   1. Dark Soy Sauce (1/2 tbsp)
   1. Sugar (2.5 teaspoons)
1. Protein (chicken/shrimp)

## Instructions

1. Make sauce (can keep in fridge) and, garlic and chilli paste
1. Cook the protein (if needed)
1. One batch: Cook garlic/chilli paste before adding in the carrots/greens
1. One batch: Add sugar, protein, noodles and sauce and coat all the noodles
1. Once the noodles are charred (not wet), add holy basil leaves before finishing
1. Cook next batch

## Modifications

1. Cook one batch at a time
1. Char the noodles
1. Cook protein separately
1. Use a wok and (flat) rice flakes
