## Ingredients

![onions](/images/recipes/legumes/legumes-lentil-curry-01.jpg)

1. Brown Lentils (3/4 cup dry)
1. Coconut Milk (half can) or Dairy-Free (e.g. Plant Based) Yogurt (3 tbsp)
1. Lentil Base
   * Red Onion (1 large)
   * Garlic (2 tbsp)
   * Ginger (1/2 cube)
1. Spices (see below)
1. Tomato paste (for Indian curry) or 2x blended tomatoes (otherwise)

## Instructions 

1. Lentils
   * Cook over 10 min or soak overnight
1. Toast almond flour and shredded coconut for 3-4 min
1. Sautee onions, garlic and ginger until brown
1. Add jerk spices until fragrant and almost a paste
1. Mix blended tomatoes or paste, and then mint/coriander/water
1. Add the coconut milk (jerk) / yogurt (curry) and boil (curry) or turn into paste (jerk)
1. Cook on high until all the water has almost evaporated
1. Add overnight lentils or cook them in
   * If too hard, add more water and set to simmer

## Modifications

1. Jerk Spices
   * Salt (1 tbsp)
   * All Spice (2 tsp)
   * Cinnamon (2 tsp)
   * Chilli Powder (or Paprika, 1 tsp)
   * Parsely (dried, 2.5 tbsp)
   * Thyme (dried, 2.5 tbsp)
1. South Indian Spices
   * Almond flour (1/4 cup)
   * Shredded coconut (2 tbsp)
   * Broken green cardomoms
   * Menthi Leaves (1/3 fenugreek cube)
   * Chilli Powder (2 tbsp)
   * Garam Masala (1 tsp)
   * Black Pepper (1 tsp)
   * 5 Mint Leaves, Cilantro, Green Chilli  


### Notes

* Lentil curries all use the same base (onions, garlic, ginger, tomatoes, coconut milk/yogurt).
* Just switch the spices for Indian / Thai lentils.
