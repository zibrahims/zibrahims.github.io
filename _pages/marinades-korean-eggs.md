## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. 1/2 cup Soy Sauce (base)
1. 1/2 cup water to dilute
1. Red Onions
1. Green Onions
1. Green and Red Chillies
1. Honey or Agave (3 tsp)
1. Minced Garlic (1 tbsp)
1. Sesame Oil (1 tbsp)
1. Sesame Seeds (1 tbsp)

## Instructions

1. Mix and add water to raise level
1. Marinate eggs for 48 hrs max
1. Add paper towel on top if eggs stick out
