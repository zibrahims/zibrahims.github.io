## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Orzo (1 cup)
1. Chicken broth
1. Onions (white or green)
1. Creamers
   1. Butter
   1. Half and half (optional)
2. Spices
   * Garlic
   * Salt
1. Chicken

## Instructions

1. Sautee onion (white/green) and garlic with butter
1. Add orzo and chicken broth (or water) to cook
1. Optional: add half and half with some butter to thicken
1. Add chicken, parmesean and spinach to finish

## Modifications

1. Half and half with butter to make extra creamy
1. Add sun dried tomatoes
1. Add cheese to make cheesy
