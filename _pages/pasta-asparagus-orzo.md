## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Orzo
1. Asparagus
1. Garlic and Breadcrumbs
1. Lemon
1. Green Onions

## Instructions

1. Cut green onion and cook
1. Add orzo + water and boil in pan. Stop when undercooked by 2 min.
1. In a separate pan, cook garlic + breadcrumbs
1. Add asparagus and cook to finish
1. Top orzo + asparagus with breadcrumb mix and lemon
