## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Buckwheat (1 cup dried)
1. Water (2 cups)
1. Egg (1x)
1. Chicken Buillon
1. Butter (1 tbsp)
1. Pilaf Vegetables
   * Chopped green onions
   * Chopped carrots
   * Sliced Mushrooms
   * Sliced Red Peppers


## Instructions

1. Make chicken stock by boiling water, butter and chicken bouillon
1. Coat buckwheat with 1 egg and lightly toast on a pan
1. Add chicken stock to buckwheat and low simmer covered for 7-10 minutes
1. Sautee pilaf vegetables in butter and add to buckwheat when simmering

## Modifications

1. Cook buckwheat alone and have with lamb/beef
