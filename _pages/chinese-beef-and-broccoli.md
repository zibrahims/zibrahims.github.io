## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Beef or Beef Substitute
1. Real Beef Marinade
   * Same as sauce, but with:
     * no dark soy sauce
     * +1 tsp baking soda
     * +1 tbsp cooking oil
1. Water (3.5 regular cups)
1. Sauce
   * Oyster Sauce (1 tbsp)
   * Light Soy Sauce (1 tbsp)
   * Dark Soy Sauce (1 tsp)
   * Water (1 tbsp)
   * Sugar (1 tsp)
   * Corn Starch (1 tsp)
1. Sesame seeds
1. Broccoli and Carrots
1. Vegetable spices
   * Garlic (2 cloves)
   * Ginger (1 small piece)
   * Green onions (2 stems)
1. Soft Tofu (optional)

## Instructions

1. Marinate real beef or use as is for plant-based, before cooking
1. Stir fry broccoli and juliened carrots with garlic, ginger and green onions
1. Add all ingreadients together and cook with sauce
1. Add sesame seeds to finish

## Modifications

1. Add soft tofu with the sauce when finishing
