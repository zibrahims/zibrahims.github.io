## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Mini or Fingerling Potatoes
1. Olive oil
1. Spices
   * Salt
   * Garlic Powder
   * Black Pepper
   * Oregano, Italiano, Parsely and/or Rosemary

## Instructions (45 min)

1. Cut potatoes in half and mix with spices + olive oil
1. Roast at 400f with skin up for 15 min
1. Flip and roast with flesh up for 15 min
1. Broil flesh side up for 5 min
