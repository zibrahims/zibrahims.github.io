## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Red Lentils (soaked for 30 min)
1. Onions, Carrots, Celery
1. Half tube (75g) of Tomato Paste
1. Walnuts
1. Crushed tomatoes (full can)
1. Spices
   * Salt
   * Oregano
   * Thyme

## Instructions

1. Soak lentils for 30 min
1. Chop onions, carrots and celery before sauteeing 
1. Add lentils, tomato paste, blended crushed tomatoes and pasta water
1. Cook, adding spices and reduce
