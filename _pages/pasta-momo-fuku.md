## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Chow Mein Noodles
1. Uncle Tammy's Chilli Oil
1. Momofuku (Sesame) Chilli Oil
1. Soy Sauce
1. Sunny Side Egg

## Instructions

1. Cook chow mein
1. Mix soy sauce, and two chilli oils
1. Top with sunny side egg
