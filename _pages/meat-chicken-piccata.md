## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Chicken Breasts (thinly cut)
1. Half and half cream
1. Flour
1. Spices
   * salt
   * pepper
   * garlic powder
1. Sauce
   * Chicken Broth (1 cup) 
   * Lemon Juice
   * Capers
1. Heavy cream

## Instructions

1. Sprinkle thin cutlets with spices and cover with flour
1. Brown cutlets on both sides
1. Remove cutlets, and in same pan make sauce and add butter
1. Stir in the cream
1. Return chicken and simmer until chicken is cooked through
