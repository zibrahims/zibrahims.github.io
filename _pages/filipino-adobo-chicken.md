## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. 3 chicken breasts or 6 boneless thighs
1. Onion
1. Regular Soy Sauce (1/3 cup)
1. Vinegar (up to 1/3 cup, must test)
1. Spices
   * Bay leaves
   * Garlic (3 tsp)
   * Black pepper
1. Brown Sugar
1. Oil
1. Optional Toppings
   * Green onions

## Instructions

1. Make marinade adding 40ml of vinegar to test acidity to your liking
1. Marinate chicken for at least 30 min (up to overnight)
1. Cut onions
1. Sear chicken
1. Heat onion and garlic
1. Add water, sugar and black pepper and simmer for 5 min
1. Add chicken and simmer each side for ~15 min until sauce thickens
1. Coat chicken in glaze and serve with green onions
