## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/condiments/condiments-pickled-onion-02.jpg)

1. Red Onions
1. Vinegar
1. Boiling Water
1. Salt



## Instructions

1. While water boils, cut red onions into thin edible slices before bottling 
1. Add salt to a half/half mix of water and vinegar before pouring into the bottle
1. Put into the fridge overnight

## Modifications

1. Other spices (garlic, peppers) for different pickling tastes
