1. Gnocchi
1. Shallots
1. Green Onions
1. Mushrooms
1. Spices
    * Salt
    * Pepper
1. Oil and/or Butter
1. Spinach

## Instructions

1. Heat overn to 425
1. On a sheet, mix oil, shallots, onions, mushrooms, salt, pepper and gnocchi
1. Cook until gnocchi and mushrooms are golden brown (15-25 min)
1. Put in a pan and mix spinach until tender
1. Make sauce by mixing: horseradish, mustard and honey
1. Mix gonicchi with light oil/butter and sauce

## Modifications

1. Add parmesean cheese on top
1. Add half and half to make creamier
