## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. 3 tempeh blocks
1. 5 cloves chopped garlic
1. 3 shallots
1. 3 tsp ginger
1. 1-2 chillies
1. Sauce
   * 4.5 tbsp soy sauce
   * 1.5 tsp salt
   * 3 tbsp water
   * 3 tbsp oyster sauce
1. Oil

## Instructions

1. Dice tempeh into cubes and fry in oil until brown 
1. Fry garlic, shallots, chillies and add sauce 
1. Throw in tempeh and coat till fully covered
1. Serve hot with rice
