## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Basmati Rice
1. Ghee
1. Spices
   * Salt
   * Diced Ginger
   * Garlic
   * Cinnamon
   * Cardamom
   * Bay Leaf
1. Optional Toppings
   * (Red) Onions
   * Green Chillis
   * Parsley

## Instructions

1. Toast rice on low with a teaspoon of ghee and pinch of salt moving to avoid burning
1. Cook in rice cooker with spices
1. Fish out bay leaf, cardamom and cinnamon
