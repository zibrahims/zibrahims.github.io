## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Thighs or Drumsticks (skin on)
1. Jerk Sauce

## Air Fryer Instructions (Thighs, 16 min)

1. In airfryer, cook for 7 min at 180C each side (14 min total)
1. Cook at 200C for 1 min on each side (+2 min total)

## Instructions (Drumsticks with Skin, 20 min)

1. In airfryer, cook for 10 min at 200C each side (20 min total)
