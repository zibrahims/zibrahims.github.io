## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Soba Noodles
1. Vegetables
   * Zucchini
   * Red Pepper
   * Greens
   * Sun Dried Tomatoe
1. Sauces
   * Hot habanero sauce
   * Naked peanut sauce

## Instructions

1. Grill vegetables while sun dried tomatoes soak in water
1. Cook soba noodles and toss in sauce

## Modifications

1. Other grilled vegetables
