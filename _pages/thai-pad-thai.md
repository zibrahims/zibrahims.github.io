## Ingredients

![sauce](/images/recipes/thai/thai-pad-thai-01.jpg)
![ingredients](/images/recipes/thai/thai-pad-thai-02.jpg)

1. Pad Thai Sauce
   1. "Pre-Made"
      * Pad Thai Sauce (2 tbsp) - Cock brand
      * Tom Yum Paste (1 tbsp)
      * Oyster Sauce (1/2 tbsp)
      * Soy Sauce (1/2 tbsp)
      * Fish Sauce (1/2 tbsp)
   1. From Scratch
      * Tamarind sauce  (1 tbsp)
      * Brown sugar (4 tbsp)
      * Oyster sauce (1.5 tbsp)
      * Fish sauce (2 tbsp)
      * Tom Yum Paste (1 tbsp)
1. Rice Noodles
1. Eggs (2)
1. Protein
   * Tofu
   * Shrimp or Chicken (thinly sliced)
1. Green Onions
1. Kefir Lime Leaves

## Instructions

1. Soak the rice noodles in boiled water for 15 min. until they get soft
1. Mix the pad thai sauce until it tastes sweet/salty (not sour). Make more than needed
1. Concurrently cook and mix the following:
   * Eggs
   * Protein
   * Roots of the green onion
1. To the mix, add and cook/coat until sauce mixed over all noodles
   * lime leaves
   * noodles
   * sauce + a bit of water (from noodles)
   * protein
1. Serve by adding the peanuts and green onions
