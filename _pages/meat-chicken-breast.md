## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Chicken Breast
1. Salt
1. Spices for Flavouring (1 choice)
   * Adobo
   * Old Bay
1. Oil

## Air Fryer Instructions

1. Marinate for at least 1 hr
1. Cook on 400F for 10 min (each side)

## Grill Pan Instructions

1. Marinate for at least 1 hr
1. Cook on grill top until seared and liquid is white
