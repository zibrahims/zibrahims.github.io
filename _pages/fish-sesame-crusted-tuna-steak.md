## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Tuna Steaks
1. Marinade (Soy Sauce + Sesame Oil)
1. Spices
   * Granulated Onion or Garlic
   * Salt
   * Pepper (optional)
   * Brown Sugar
   * Sesame Seeds
1. Optional Toppings
   * Green Onions
   * Smoked Salt

## Instructions

1. Marinate for 10 min either side
1. Oiled pan on high and grill each side for 50s
1. Slice and serve with green onions + smoked salt
