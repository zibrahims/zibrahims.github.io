## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. 500g ground chicken/turkey 
1. 3 tbsp breadcrums
1. Onion
1. Garlic (4 cloves)
1. Ginger (1 piece)
1. Spices
   * 1 tsp salt
   * 1/2 tsp black pepper
   * 3 green chillis
   * 1 tsp red chilli
   * 1.5 tsp garam masala
   * 1.5 tsp coriander
   * 1.5 tsp cumin
1. Oil for frying in pan


## Instructions

1. Blend onion, garlic, ginger and chillis
1. Mix spices, breadcrumbs and blend into ground meat
1. Roll into kebabs
1. Fry in oil or airfry
