## Ingredients (2 persons)

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. 1 cup dried macaroni elbows
1. Sauce
   * 300g of soft tofu
   * 1/3 cup nutritional yeast
   * Dijon mustard (1 tbsp)
   * Garlic powder (2 tsp)
   * Onion powder (1 tsp)
   * Salt (1 tsp)
   * Paprika (1/2 tsp)
   * Lemon juice (1 tsp)
   * Sugar (1/2 tsp)
1. Butter (vegan or otherwise, 1 tbsp)
1. Optional 
   * Diced mushrooms
   * Diced spinach
   * Diced chicken

## Instructions

1. Blend sauce while macaroni is cooking
1. Cook diced mushrooms and chicken
1. Add sauce and butter to macaroni 
1. Mix with mushrooms, spinach and chicken
