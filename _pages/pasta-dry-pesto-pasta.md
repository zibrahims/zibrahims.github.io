## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Fussili
1. Pesto
1. Spices
   * Truffle Salt
   * Pepper
   * Oregano
1. Oil
1. Chicken
1. Optional Toppings
   * Cherry Tomatoes
   * Sun Dried Tomatoes
   * Parsley

## Instructions

1. Cook pasta and warm up chicken on the side
1. Mix pesto, spices
1. Add terroni chilli oil

## Modifications

1. Use angel hair instead of fussili
1. Cook pasta in a sauteed base of garlic and shallots
1. Add cooked cherry tomatoes
