## Ingredients (2 servings)

1. Cheddar Powder (1/4 cup)
1. Elbow Macaroni (1 cup dry)
1. Butter (1 tbsp)
1. Milk (1/3 cup)
1. Mushrooms
1. Spinach 
1. Chicken


## Instructions 

1. Boil macaroni pasta
1. In a pot, mix cheddar powder, butter and milk to make the sauce
1. Dice and cook the mushroom, spinach and chicken
1. Add the pasta and diced items into the sauce mix
