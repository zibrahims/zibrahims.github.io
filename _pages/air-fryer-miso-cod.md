## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Filet and deboned black cod (sable flesh)
1. 1/4 cup sake (rice wine)
1. 2 tbsp mirin
1. 1/3 cup white miso paste
1. 1/4 cup of sugar
1. Green onions

## Instructions

1. Cut fish into small portions (big portions cook very slow) 
1. Heat sake and mirin to cook out the alcohol
1. Mix in miso paste and sugar and heat
1. Cool to room temperature
1. Marinate for at least 24 hrs to 2 days
1. Air fry for 10-12 min
1. Garnish with green onions on top
