## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Ice cubes 
1. Tempranillo wine (375mL, Beronia) 
1. Cognac Orange Liquer (max. 1/8 cup, Grand Mariner)
1. Orange Juice (1/2 cup)
1. Agave or Maple Syrup (2 tbsp)
1. Sparkling Water (min. 1 cup)
1. Sliced lemons, apples and/or orange


## Instructions

1. Mix
1. Less cognac if you wish for it to be less alchy
1. More sparkling water for lighter drink
