## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Juliened Vegetables
   * 1x Carrot
   * 1/2 English Cucumber
   * 1x Jicama
   * 2x Roma Tomatoes w/ seeds removed
   * 1x Daikon (Lo Bok)
1. Fried Toppings
   * Taro Root (spiraled or juliened)
   * Vermicelli
1. Canola Oil (for frying)
1. Garnishes (optional)
   * Crushed peanuts
   * Sprouts or microgreends
   * Flower Petals
1. Salad Dressing 
   * Umeboshi (1 cup)
   * Rice Vinegar (1/2 cup)
   * Agave or Yuzu Honey (2 tbsp)
   * Ginger (1/2 tbsp)
   * 4 raspberries (enough to turn dressing red)

## Instructions

1. Blend salad dressing and keep in a bottle
1. Juliene all the vegetables
1. Fry the taro root and vermicelli
1. Assemble salad
   1. Lay salad dressing at the bottom of the plate
   1. Assemble the juliened vegetables in a pyramid
   1. Add fried taro root and fried vermicelli on top
   1. Add garnish
1. Save extra dressing and juliened vegetables
