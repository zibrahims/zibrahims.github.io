## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Juliened Vegetables
   * 1x Carrot
   * 1/2 English Cucumber
   * 1x Jicama
   * 2x Roma Tomatoes w/ seeds removed
   * 1x Daikon (Lo Bok)
1. Fried Toppings
   * Taro Root (spiraled or juliened)
   * Vermicelli
1. Canola Oil (for frying)
1. Garnishes (optional)
   * Crushed peanuts
   * Sprouts or microgreends
   * Flower Petals
1. Raspberry Salad Dressing 
   * LARGE handful of raspberries 
   * Umeboshi (3/4 tbsp)
   * Rice Vinegar (1 tbsp)
   * Mirin (1.5 tbsp)
   * Agave (0.5 tbsp)
   * Ginger (1/2 inch ginger root)
   * Water to dilute 

## Instructions

1. Blend salad dressing and keep in a bottle
1. Juliene all the vegetables but SEPARATE cucumber/tomatoes/daikon from taro
1. *Deep* fry the taro root and vermicelli
1. Assemble salad
   1. Lay salad dressing at the bottom of the plate
   1. Add fried vermicelli in centre
   1. Assemble the juliened vegetables in a pyramid around the virmecilli
   1. Add garnish
1. Save extra dressing and juliened vegetables
