## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Garlic (1 whole) and Garlic Powder (1/4 tsp)
1. Chicken Broth (1/2 cup)
1. Heavy Whipping Cream (1 cup)
1. Lemon Juice (1/2 tsp)

## Instructions

1. Sautee garlic
1. Add broth, lemon juice, garlic powder and heavy cream
