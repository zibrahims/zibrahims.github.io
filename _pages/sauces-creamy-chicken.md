## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Garlic (1 whole) and Garlic Powder (1/4 tsp)
1. Chicken Broth (1/4 cup)
1. Half & Half Cream (1 cup)
1. Lemon Juice (1/2 tsp)
1. Parsely

## Instructions

1. Sautee garlic
1. Add broth, lemon juice, garlic powder and half and half
1. Add parsely
1. Optional: add old bay spice to chicken 

## Modifications

1. Ratio of broth to half and half should be to make the sauce creamy
