## Ingredients

![ingredients](/images/recipes/bangla/bangla-dahl-01.jpg)

1. Cauliflower
1. Potatoes
1. Ginger, Garlic, Onions
1. Tomatoes
1. Spices
   * Tumeric
   * Coriander
   * Cumin
1. Salt, Coriander, Garam Masala

## Instructions

1. Cut the potatoes and cauliflower
1. Roast the outside of the potatoes while sauteeing the onions, garlic and ginger
1. Once soft, add the spices and lightly roasted potatoes into boiling water
1. Once the potatoes are partially cooked, add in the cauliflower
1. Once the cauliflower is soft enough to eat, add coriander, garam masala and salt

## Modifications

1. Add tomatoes if desired to the onion, garlic and ginger mix
